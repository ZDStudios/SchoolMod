Every tab, one timetable.

Ten tools that used to live in ten browser tabs, now sitting in one app that opens in under a second.

DASH

Dashboard

Your day at a glance: next class, what's due, cards to review, quick actions.

ASST

AI Assistant

A streaming study chat powered by your own Claude or ChatGPT account — one click to connect, nothing to configure.

NBK

Notebooks

Drop in PDFs, Word docs or notes and chat with them — answers are grounded in your own sources, with citations.

CRD

Flashcards

Generate a deck from any topic or your own notes, then review it on a spaced-repetition schedule.

SEQ

SEQTA

Timetable, assessments, grades, homework and notices, signed in through your school's own Microsoft login.

365

Microsoft 365

One-click launch for Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Teams and OneNote, plus your recent OneDrive files.

MATH

Mathspace & Education Perfect

Launch either platform with a worked-solution tutor and an on-demand revision sheet for whatever you're stuck on.

BELL

Bell times

A live current-period tracker with a countdown to the next bell, carried over from the original SchoolMod.

FOC

Focus, to-do & scratchpad

A Pomodoro timer that tracks daily focus minutes, a task list, and a notepad that saves itself.

CMD

Command palette

Ctrl+K (or ⌘K on a Mac) jumps anywhere in the app or runs an action without touching the mouse.

    First launch note — these builds aren't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen or macOS Gatekeeper will flag them. On Windows choose More info → Run anyway; on a Mac, right-click the app and choose Open.

    Nothing leaves your laptop

    SchoolMod runs locally. Your notes, decks, login sessions and AI conversations stay on your device — there's no SchoolMod server in the middle, because one was never built. The Claude and ChatGPT connections talk to your own account directly, and SEQTA or Microsoft sign-ins go straight to your school or Microsoft, not through us.
