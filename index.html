Study

AI Assistant

A streaming study chat that runs on your own Claude subscription through Claude Code.

Notebooks

Chat with your own PDFs, Word documents and notes. Answers carry inline citations, and one click makes a summary or study guide.

Flashcards

Generate cards from a topic or your notes, then review them with SM-2 spaced repetition.

School accounts

SEQTA

Timetable, assessments, homework and notices, with your name and student photo.

Microsoft 365

One-click launch for OneNote, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Teams, Outlook, OneDrive and To Do, plus your recent OneDrive files.

Mathspace

Launch the app, get worked solutions from the AI, and keep a practice streak.

Education Perfect

Launch the app and get a study coach that builds revision sheets.

Bell times

A live period countdown, including Trinity's Tuesday and Friday timetables.

Every day

Dashboard

Your name, next lesson, due assessments and cards to review on one screen, with quick actions.

Command palette

Press Ctrl or ⌘ + K to jump anywhere or run an action. Try it on this page.

Focus tools

A Pomodoro timer that counts your daily focus minutes, a task list and a scratchpad that saves as you type.

Appearance

Light and dark themes, 8 accent colours and a custom frameless window.

Runs on your computer.

  • No SchoolMod server. Your notes, cards and settings stay on your machine.

  • Your own Claude login. The assistant talks to your Claude subscription through the Claude Code CLI.

  • No cloud search. Document retrieval and spaced repetition are plain TypeScript, with no vector database.

  • A study tool, nothing sneakier. It does not answer quizzes for you or get around school filters.

First launch

  • Builds are unsigned, so you will see a warning once.
  • Windows: choose More info, then Run anyway.
  • macOS: right-click the app and choose Open.
  • The portable build unpacks itself on every launch, so use the installer if you can.

Connect what you use

  • Claude AI: one-click CLI install and login.
  • SEQTA: single sign-on, the MCP server, or a direct login.
  • Microsoft 365: an Entra ID app with device-code sign-in.
  • All of it is optional and lives in Settings.
